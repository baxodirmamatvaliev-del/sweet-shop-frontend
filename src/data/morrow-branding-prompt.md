# Morrow Coffee cup edits

Tool: built-in imagegen. Each of the 20 selected product photos was edited separately using the existing Morrow Coffee logo as an artwork reference, then exported to a WebP with a maximum edge of 640px.

## Shared prompt

Use case: precise-object-edit. Image 1 is the product photo edit target. Image 2 is logo artwork reference ONLY; use the wordmark at its top, ignore blank area. Replace ALL existing MEGA/MGC branding on the drink cup with the exact MORROW [tilted cup M icon] COFFEE logo from image 2, centered on the front where original mark was, about 60% cup width, printed realistically with cylindrical perspective. Exact text MORROW COFFEE. No old branding remnants. Warm-white ink on dark clear coffee cups; dark cocoa ink on pale drinks or paper cups. Keep the existing cup color/material and shape, drink contents, ice/foam/toppings, lighting, white background, original square full-product framing unchanged. Do not add labels, objects, captions, people or watermarks. Only edit the brand mark. Output a single square edited product photo.

Each call also supplied the drink name. For House Milk Latte and Orzo Latte, the prompt additionally requested removal of the floating ONLY ICE and ORZO/CRASTAN badges and dark cocoa ink over the pale cup center.
